Daily automation

The scheduler runs daily at 09:00 UTC. It creates a topic-specific caption, rotates through your configured public image URLs, and uses Redis to prevent duplicate posts if the scheduled request is retried.

To enable it, configure Meta credentials, REDIS_URL, CRON_SECRET, INSTAGRAM_AUTO_POST_ENABLED=true, and public HTTPS image URLs in INSTAGRAM_DAILY_IMAGE_URLS (comma-separated) or INSTAGRAM_DEFAULT_IMAGE_URL. Reconnect Instagram after Redis is configured so the account token is stored securely.
